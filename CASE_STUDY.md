# ExportFlow AI — Product Case Study

## Overview

ExportFlow AI is a portfolio prototype exploring how a structured-data review workflow could help Indian MSME exporters compare related trade documents. It compares commercial invoice and packing-list data, surfaces mismatches, checks basic arithmetic, and creates a downloadable CSV report.

**Role:** Product framing, requirements, prototype implementation, and planned evaluation  
**Status:** Working portfolio prototype  
**Tools:** Python, Streamlit, Pandas

## Problem hypothesis

Export operations users may need to compare key details across documents before a shipment moves forward. Manual review can be repetitive, and discrepancies may create avoidable follow-up work.

This is a hypothesis to validate through conversations with exporters or freight-forwarding professionals, not a finding from completed customer research.

## Target user

An operations executive at a small or medium-sized exporter who checks shipment documents and coordinates corrections with colleagues or trading partners.

## Proposed solution

A lightweight review tool that:
1. Loads a sample shipment or accepts structured invoice and packing-list data.
2. Compares supplied fields, including invoice number, currency, quantity, unit price, and total value.
3. Flags differences and missing values.
4. Checks whether quantity × unit price agrees with the stated total where the required numeric fields are available.
5. Exports the field-level results for a human to review.

## User journey

Open the app → load sample data or upload CSV/JSON documents → compare fields → inspect summary and source values → download the CSV report → follow up manually.

## Key product decisions

- **Show the source values:** users can see the invoice and packing-list values side by side.
- **Human in the loop:** the tool flags possible issues; it does not approve shipments.
- **Use explicit rules for exact fields:** deterministic comparison is suitable for equality and basic arithmetic checks in this MVP.
- **State limitations clearly:** the prototype does not use PDF/OCR extraction or a connected language model and does not certify compliance.

## Current features

- Synthetic sample shipment to try the workflow.
- CSV/JSON input.
- Field-level comparison and status summary.
- Basic arithmetic validation.
- Downloadable CSV report.

## Evaluation plan

Test with synthetic documents containing both matching fields and deliberately seeded discrepancies. Record:
- Number of seeded discrepancies.
- Number detected and missed.
- Number of clean fields incorrectly flagged.
- Time taken by a tester to complete the review.

These tests have not been reported as completed results. No customer impact or time savings are claimed at this stage.

## Limitations

- No completed customer research is claimed.
- No PDF/OCR extraction in the current build.
- Comparison guidance is rule-based, not generated by a connected LLM.
- The prototype does not check official customs requirements or certify compliance.
- Synthetic data is used for demonstration; human review is required.

## Next steps

1. Interview 3–5 export operations users and revise assumptions.
2. Test usability with realistic but anonymized sample documents.
3. Add PDF/OCR extraction with source snippets.
4. Add editable field corrections and audit history.
5. Evaluate whether an LLM helps with ambiguous descriptions and explanations.

## Demo script (60–90 seconds)

“ExportFlow AI is a portfolio prototype exploring how exporters could review cross-document information more systematically. I load a sample shipment and compare the invoice and packing list. The app flags that the quantity differs—100 versus 90—and shows the total-value difference. I can inspect the field-level results and download a CSV for follow-up. This MVP uses transparent rules on structured data; the next step is to validate the workflow with export operations users and explore source-grounded document extraction.”
